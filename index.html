What We Plan For

Three reasons landowners call us

Most projects are a blend of these — that's exactly how we plan them, together, before a saw ever starts.

Wildlife Habitat Cuts

Opening the canopy to bring browse, mast, and cover back down to the forest floor — built around the game you hunt and the habitat you want to see thrive for the next twenty years.

Revenue Timber Harvests

Mature, marketable timber converted into income, harvested and hauled with care for the trees, soil, and access left behind.

Viewshed Cuts

Selective removal that opens up the view you're after — a ridge, a pond, a valley — without stripping a hillside bare.

There's no cost and no obligation to find out which one fits your land. That conversation happens before anything else does.

Working Nearby? Let's Talk.
If we're already working near you, your project starts with a head start.

Access roads, equipment mobilization, and setup make up a real share of any harvest's cost. When our crew is already at work on a property nearby, a neighboring landowner's project can often be coordinated alongside it — lowering those costs from the start.
